NirmanVaani — Official Pitch Deck

Slide 1 • Title & Hook

NirmanVaani (निर्माणवाणी): The Multilingual Citizen-to-Policy Bridge

Track: Digital Public Infrastructure | Builder: Abhinav Kumar (solo). Transforming fragmented citizen complaints in 22 Indian languages into explainable, ranked infrastructure allocations for ministries.

Slide 2 • The Problem

The $1.4 Trillion Civic Gap in Infrastructure Spending

Citizen feedback sits in siloed paper registers, unmonitored helplines, and social media. Budgets get allocated where political noise is loudest rather than where infrastructure deficits and population vulnerability are highest.

Slide 3 • The Solution

Voice-First Intake + Gemini AI Structuring + Explainable Ranking

Citizens simply talk into their phone in Bhojpuri, Bengali, Hindi, or Tamil. Gemini translates, categorizes the sector (Roads, Water, Health), extracts geo-entities, and feeds an open mathematical priority scoring engine.

Slide 4 • Explainable Priority Algorithm (Not a Black Box)

Defensible Formula: Score = w1·Demand + w2·InfraGap + w3·PopReach

Policymakers control policy weight sliders dynamically. Real Census data and PMGSY road connectivity metrics prevent noise from overpowering underserved rural communities.

Slide 5 • Technical Architecture

Built on Managed Google Cloud & Web Standards

Vite + React / TypeScript frontend, Cloud Functions / FastAPI, Firestore citizen store, BigQuery analytics, and Gemini API structuring pipeline.

Slide 6 • Grounding in Reality

Real Datasets from data.gov.in

Census population, PMGSY unconnected road rates, Jal Jeevan tap deficits, and Rural Health Mission PHC vacancy data for Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, and Maharashtra.

Slide 7 • Public Transparency & Trust

Citizen Tracking & Community Endorsement

Citizens can track their ticket ID lifecycle and upvote neighbor complaints ("Me Too"), preventing spam while building genuine civic trust in public governance.

Slide 8 • Scalability & DPI Roadmap

Expanding Across All 766 Districts of India

Deployable as a standard Digital Public Good (DPG) with Open API integration into state CM Helplines and Ministry of Panchayati Raj systems.

Slide 9 • Judging Criteria Alignment

100% Alignment with Google Cloud Hackathon Rubric

Problem-Solution Fit (20%), AI/Technical Execution (25%), Depth & Reach (20%), Impact Potential (15%), Deployability & Scalability (20%).
